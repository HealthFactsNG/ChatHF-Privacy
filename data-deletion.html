ChatHF Privacy policy

Delete your ChatHF data

ChatHF by HealthFacts

You can ask us to delete some of your ChatHF data, such as your chat history, and keep your account. To delete everything, delete your account instead.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

  1. Open the data deletion request form using the button below.
  2. Enter the email address you use to sign in to ChatHF.
  3. Choose what you want deleted, then submit the form.
  4. We'll email that address to confirm the request came from you. Reply to confirm.
  5. We delete the data and email you when it's done.

Open the deletion request form

We'll never ask for your password. Anyone who asks for it isn't us.

What you can ask us to delete

Chat history
Every question you've asked, the answers ChatHF gave, and the sources it cited.
Saved items
The articles and videos you bookmarked.
Consult requests
Consultations you requested, including the type, length, quoted price and any notes you wrote.
Profile name
The name on your profile. You can also clear it yourself in the app, under Profile → Settings.

What we keep

Your email address and a hashed copy of your password stay on file, because without them your account can't work. Everything you don't choose to delete stays too. To remove these as well, delete your account.

How long it takes

  • We act on confirmed requests within 30 days, usually much sooner.
  • Once deleted, the data is removed from our live database right away. Copies in backups, if any, are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.
  • We keep a record of your request (your email, what you asked for and when) for up to 12 months to show that we handled it. Then it's deleted.

Delete your whole account

This deletes your account and all of its data immediately. It can't be undone.

  • In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  • On the web: chathfapi.healthfacts.ng/account/delete
