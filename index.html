Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

ChatHF answers health questions from published health writing and shows you its sources. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it goes to, and how to delete it.

Summary

  • We collect your email, an optional name, your questions and answers, the articles you save, and any consult requests you make.
  • Your questions are sent to OpenAI to produce answers. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • The app has no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs, and it doesn't access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time, in the app or on the web.

1. Who we are

ChatHF is operated by HealthFacts (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at the address in Contact us.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

Account details
Your email address, password and, if you add one, your name. We never store your password itself, only a one-way (bcrypt) hash of it.
Questions and answers
The questions you ask, the answers ChatHF gives, and the articles and videos it cited. These are saved to your chat history.
Saved items
The articles and videos you bookmark: their title and link.
Consult requests
If you request a consultation, we record the type of consult, the session length, the price you were quoted and any note you write.

Information collected automatically

Technical data
Our servers see your IP address and basic request details when the app connects to them. We use these to deliver the service, limit repeated requests and investigate errors. We don't build profiles from them.
On your device
The app keeps your sign-in token and basic profile (email and name) in its private storage so you stay signed in. Signing out or deleting your account removes them.

What we don't collect

ChatHF has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files, and it doesn't take payments in the app.

3. Health information

Your questions may reveal information about your health or someone else’s. We treat everything you type as sensitive. We use it only to answer you and to keep your history, and we never use it for advertising.

Please don’t include details that identify you or other people, such as full names, phone numbers or addresses, in your questions.

ChatHF provides general health information. It is not a diagnosis and not a substitute for advice from a qualified health professional. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • OpenAI processes the text of your questions, and the passages retrieved to answer them, to produce ChatHF's answers. Under OpenAI's API terms this data is not used to train its models. OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days to monitor abuse before deleting it. Your email and name are not sent to OpenAI.
  • Hosting providers run the servers that store our database. They process data only on our instructions.
  • Health professionals: if you request a consult, the details of that request are shared with the professional who handles it.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or others.
  • Business transfers: if ChatHF is merged or sold, your information may pass to the new owner under this policy.

6. International transfers

OpenAI processes data in the United States, so your questions may be transferred outside Nigeria. We rely on the safeguards permitted by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 for these transfers, including our provider's contractual commitments.

7. How long we keep it

  • We keep your account details, chat history, saved items and consult requests for as long as you have an account.
  • When you delete your account, all of it is deleted from our live database immediately. Copies in backups, if any, are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.
  • We keep technical logs only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.
  • We may keep information longer where the law requires it.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all of the data linked to it at any time. You can do this two ways:

  1. In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  2. On the web: if you no longer have the app, go to chathfapi.healthfacts.ng/account/delete and sign in with your email and password.

Deletion takes effect immediately and permanently removes your profile, chat history, saved items and consult requests. It can't be undone. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

9. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and similar laws where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the information we hold about you
  • correct information that is wrong
  • have your information deleted
  • object to or restrict how we use it
  • receive a copy in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time

You can change your name and password and delete your account in the app. For anything else, contact us. We'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or your local data protection authority.

10. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, sign-in attempts are rate limited, and access to our servers is restricted. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you and the regulator as the law requires.

11. Children

ChatHF is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
